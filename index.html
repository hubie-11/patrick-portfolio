•  Skills & Services

Shopify Design & Development

I design and develop custom Shopify experiences for brands that want a storefront that feels polished, flexible, and easy to manage.

My work includes full Shopify builds, theme updates, custom sections, landing pages, product page improvements, collection templates, cart updates, and mobile UX refinements. Whether you’re launching something new or improving an existing site, I help turn your goals into a clean, conversion-focused Shopify experience.

Custom Themes, Sections & Shopify 2.0 Updates

I build Shopify themes and custom sections with long-term usability in mind.

That means giving your team more control in the theme editor, adding flexible content blocks, improving page templates, and making sure your site can evolve without requiring code changes for every small update.

I also help with Shopify 2.0 migrations, outdated theme upgrades, bug fixes, and rebuilding older customizations on newer, more stable theme frameworks.

App Integrations, Tracking & Shopify Functions

I help Shopify apps and technical integrations feel like a natural part of your site, not an awkward add-on.This includes setup, styling, troubleshooting, and customization for apps related to subscriptions, reviews, email/SMS capture, product options, filtering, search, cart functionality, and more.

I also help with custom tracking and pixel implementation, including Meta, TikTok, Google, Amazon Ads, Klaviyo, and other marketing or analytics scripts. For more advanced Shopify needs, I can build custom Shopify Functions for discounts, checkout logic, delivery customization, order routing, and other business-specific rules.

I’m also available for ongoing Shopify support, including new feature development, QA, theme improvements, and technical fixes as your site grows.
