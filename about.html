•  Biography

Hi, I’m Patrick — a Shopify focused frontend developer with over 9 years of professional experience. Currently based in San Diego, CA 🌊☀️.

I have over 9 years of professional experience in ecommerce design and development, with a focus on building, customizing, and improving Shopify storefronts for growing brands.

My work sits at the intersection of design, development, and practical problem solving. I help brands update outdated themes, build custom Shopify sections, improve product and collection pages, integrate apps, troubleshoot technical issues, and create storefronts that are both polished and easy to manage.

I care a lot about clean design, strong UX, and building things in a way that actually makes sense for the client long term. Whether I’m working from a full design file, improving an existing theme, or helping scope out the best path forward, my goal is always to make the site feel better for both the customer and the team managing it.

I also pride myself on being reliable, communicative, and easy to work with. I know a lot of ecommerce projects involve moving parts, changing priorities, and tight timelines, so I try to keep things clear, organized, and collaborative from start to finish.
